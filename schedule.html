Team Blad on tour

The Ravens women compete as Team Blad: Alyssa Blad (skip), Amelia McIntosh (third), Camille Ditty (second), Jordan Graham (lead) and Norah Smith (fifth), coached by Jamie Sinclair.

Men's team · U25 tour

Team Nicholls on tour

The Ravens men's lineup also competes as Team Nicholls in under-25 (U25) men's events: Owen Nicholls (skip), Jordan McNamara (third), Phil Burgess (second), Jacob Clarke (lead) and Jack Ragan (fifth).

Their latest event was the COMCO Cash Spiel in Stroud, where they reached the quarterfinals and finished tied for fifth. Earlier this fall they were runners-up at the Full Line Ag Curling Stadium SaskTour Series in Swift Current, losing the final 7–6 in an extra end.

Next up is the Challenge de Curling Desjardins de Charlevoix in Clermont, Que., November 26–29, 2026. The 16-team men's field plays a triple knockout for a $28,000 purse.

The draw schedule hasn't been posted yet. Game times and opponents will appear on the CurlingZone event schedule.

Full Line Ag Curling Stadium SaskTour Series5–3 · Runner-up

Swift Current, Sask., October 23–25, 2026. Team Nicholls came through the triple knockout to the playoffs, beat Team Jacobson 5–2 in the quarterfinal and Team Knapp 5–4 in the semifinal, then lost a thrilling final to Team Schneider. Down 6–3, they scored three in the eighth end to force an extra end, where Schneider won it 7–6.
